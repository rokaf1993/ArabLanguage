Privacy Policy

Arabic Flashcards app and Halaqa teacher dashboard · Last updated 25 November 2026

In short: we collect only what the app needs to work: your email to sign in, a username, and your learning progress. We don't show adverts, we don't use tracking or analytics tools, and we never sell your information. You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Arabic Flashcards (also called Halaqa) and the Halaqa teacher dashboard are run by Rokad Enterprise (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the information described here. Contact us at hello@rokadenterprise.ltd.

What we collect and why

InformationWhy we need it
Email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in, and to send account emails (confirming your email, resetting your password). Passwords are stored securely hashed by our sign-in provider; we can never see them.
Username and points scoreShown on leaderboards and to the members and teacher of any group you join.
Learning progressWhich words you know, are learning or got wrong, your daily goal and streak, lesson scores, and when and for how long you used the app. This keeps your progress across devices and lets the teacher of a group you join follow your progress.
GroupsThe groups you create or join, and their name and join code.
Things you write or sendNotes you send to your group, reports you send about a word (for example “the sound is wrong”), and, for teachers, lessons, grammar notes and words you add.
Daily word email (optional)If you turn this on in Settings, the email address you choose for it.
On your phone onlyYour settings (sound, pronunciation guide, daily goal), saved cards and a copy of your progress are kept on your device so the app works quickly and offline. This stays on your phone and is removed if you uninstall the app or reset your progress.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, device identifiers or payment details, and the app contains no advertising or third-party tracking. Word pronunciations are recordings built into the app; the optional spoken pronunciation uses your phone's own text-to-speech.

Who can see your information

  • Other users can see your username and points on leaderboards.
  • Teachers can see the progress, notes and word reports of students in groups they run, on the Halaqa teacher dashboard and the Halaqa Teacher website. If you join a group, its teacher can see your learning progress.
  • Group members can see notes posted to their group.
  • Service providers who run the app for us: Supabase (account sign-in, database and account emails, with data stored in the United Kingdom/EU), Expo (builds the app) and Google Play (distributes it). They process data only on our instructions.

We never sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers. We would disclose information only if the law requires it.

Our legal basis

Under UK and EU data protection law (UK GDPR / GDPR), we use your information to provide the service you signed up for (contract), and for features you choose to turn on, such as the daily word email (consent, which you can withdraw in Settings at any time).

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your sign-in details, profile, progress, group memberships, notes and word reports are deleted straight away. Lessons, grammar notes or words a teacher added for a group stay available to that group, no longer linked to the teacher's account.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and its data. You can also email hello@rokadenterprise.ltd from the address on your account and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. Email hello@rokadenterprise.ltd. If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

Security

Information travels between the app and our servers encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and access to the database is restricted by account. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you.

Age

Arabic Flashcards and the Halaqa teacher dashboard are intended for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
